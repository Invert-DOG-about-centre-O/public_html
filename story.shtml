Our Story

CUEiMS was founded in 2016 in the aftermath of a series of events where actions by mathematicians and the (mis)use of mathematical work caused widespread societal harm. Many students and academics came to be of the view that a contributing factor to these events was a lack of formal ethical training for mathematicians. In an age of information where mathematicians and mathematical tools had become much sought after, those with the power to create useful mathematics also needed the expertise to produce and wield these responsibly. In particular, there was need for discourse on the specific ethical issues that pertain to the unique technical aspects of a practising mathematician's work. CUEiMS was formed to fill this gap.
